## Insights that must survive simplification

### 1. Responsibility and working state are different

A durable duty ledger says what remains owed and who may accept completion. It
does not supply the observations, live explanations or discriminating next
action needed to satisfy that duty. Preserving only the ledger can produce
reliable records of failure without a stronger solver.

R2's essential loop is:

```text
consequential unresolved observation
  → discriminating experiment or supported shared-cause repair
  → replay the original invocation and affected retained controls
  → update the working frontier
  → evidence-supported continuation, handoff or explicit blocked state
```

### 2. A summary is an index, not the evidence

Retain original available bytes, invocation channels, identities and incomplete
capture boundaries. Exact recall is not new execution and should not require
repeating side effects. Save runnable probes and necessary data in the public
workspace; automatic output archival does not preserve every temporary file.

### 3. A green command is not a closed obligation

An outer exit of zero, a narrow matching batch or a candidate-authored validator
does not establish inner execution, coverage, or closure of older known
differences. Keep counterexamples and passing regressions available across
edits; independent verifier evidence is the acceptance boundary.

### 4. Keep smart work at the edges and a deterministic normative core

The pure reducer owns authority, responsibility, evidence coordinates and
recognized finality. It does not interpret arbitrary output, select experiments,
judge scores or learn policies. The current working frontier is model guidance,
not an automatic semantic oracle. Adding more kernel states or mandatory review
ceremony is not evidence of improvement.

### 5. Opportunity is not an obligation to waste time

Six hours is an absolute per-instance ceiling, not a minimum duration. Do not
force completed tasks to stay busy, use a large finite sentinel for unlimited
execution, or hide a renewed deadline inside recovery. Individual operations,
resources, cancellation and accounting remain bounded and observable.

### 6. Separate engineering, measurement and authority

Scripted providers can validate actual native Session/process/ledger behavior.
They cannot demonstrate real-model learning. A valid partial score is a
measurement, not task completion. Complete paired measurements can support a
decision; they do not grant deployment by themselves.

For future separately frozen promotion protocols: compare each task's mean over
its fixed repeats, require no task-level regression and at least one strictly
positive improvement, preserve established full passes, and require safety plus
complete development/confirmation evidence. All ties do not promote. Do not
relabel inspected tasks as unseen confirmation or post-hoc readmission as
preregistered evidence.

### 7. Whole-cohort liveness is part of experiment design

The 2026-10-12 cohort lost 31 unadmitted assignments after a credential expired.
One successful access check would not establish the credential's usable lifetime
for a sequential cohort. Keep the original failure; check credential validity
and the bounded cancellation/receipt path before a new independent launch.
Do not turn absent grading into a synthetic zero or claim an acknowledgement
that was never recorded.

## Publication checks and limitations

The current task/kernel package regressions and paired controller tests were
rerun before publication; Core, SDK and driver typechecks passed. The full
repository check is not claimed green: the inherited `Reflect.apply` usage at
`research/programbench/endpoint.test.ts:197` fails the restricted-global lint
rule, also on the unmodified base. The old V1 working tree additionally has the
retained deadline-only strategy typing mismatch, and its old host Bun crashed
in the workspace-wide pre-push typecheck.

Historical committed refs are published without editing their sources to make
today's workspace hook pass. The local pre-push hook is explicitly skipped for
this archival publication after the focused checks and redacted secret audit;
no hook configuration or remote branch history is rewritten. Publishing a ref
does not certify every old package against the current toolchain. Uncommitted
V1 changes and private artifact directories are not part of this publication.
