## Intervention

R2's reusable contribution is an evidence-led control loop: unresolved observations choose the next discriminating action; coherent repairs are checked against the original invocation and affected controls; retained evidence and work survive context refresh; self-checks do not authorize completion.

`ProContractTask.evidenceMethod` expresses this as six fixed clauses. It asks for a small durable working frontier in ordinary workspace files or executable probes. It does not prescribe a reporting format, minimum work duration, test count, planner, reviewer or new authority. The frontier is model-controlled working state, not a new kernel truth predicate. A model's claim of resolution never becomes attestation.

The two-arm experiment compares this candidate against recovered R2 v5 method bytes (`b382202c...`) plus the identical fixed-task safety suffix. Both use the same frozen task-only V2 runtime and public observation retention. This isolates the entire instruction-method intervention on V2, not the normative reducer or the old R2 binary.

## Frozen design

- Nine original research-exposed ProgramBench instances; two fixed repeats per arm, 36 original allocations.
- Luna/max; three concurrent originals; alternating arm-first order by instance, reversed in repeat two. No admission to repeat two before repeat one settles.
- Per original: one six-hour deadline, shared across checkpoints and final independent verification. Provider/tool/request/money count caps are explicitly null. A completed task stops early.
- Same public inputs, pinned rootfs/image, tools, resource division and private official grader in both arms. Solver 4 CPU/8 GiB, host/tool memory 2/6 GiB; grader 4 CPU/8 GiB.
- Method proposals, replacements and RSI are disabled and independently checked in the issuer ledger. The pure responsibility reducer is unchanged.
- Official final feedback is sealed. Unknown/invalid scores are not zero, not retried and not pooled; safely classified invalid-score endpoints may allow independent slots to continue. Execution faults or unknown cleanup fail closed.
- Task-Pareto: exact task means over fixed repeats, no task regression and at least one strict improvement, full-pass protection and complete evidence. All ties fail. This exposed development study alone cannot authorize deployment; independent confirmation and safety remain required.

The historical nine-task R2 scores are descriptive background, not the contemporaneous control. Original Luna raw provenance remains incomplete; this experiment does not reconstruct it or reopen old cohorts.
